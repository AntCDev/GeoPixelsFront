⚠️ Canvas Fingerprinting Detected

Your browser appears to have Canvas Fingerprinting protection enabled. While this is an excellent privacy feature for many websites, it specifically introduces deliberate "noise" into all of our canvas elements, which affects how our pixel art application functions.

This protection can lead to visual errors or incorrect behavior in several of our tools such as:

  • The Inspector tool might show a pixel is empty or give the wrong user's information.
  • Error highlighting for ghost images will give false positives.
  • The Eyedropper tool will ocassionally pick up the wrong colors.
  • The ability to report users will be disabled to prevent reporting the wrong user due to noisy data.

The issue here is purely technical: the introduced noise directly compromises the integrity of the pixel data on the canvas, which is fundamental to our application's core functionality, rather than us requiring more information about you.

For the best and most accurate experience, we recommend temporarily disabling this protection for our site. You can review our full Privacy Policy here.

How to Disable (Site-Specific):

For Mozilla Firefox:
  1. Click the Shields icon to the left of the address bar.
  2. Toggle the 'Enhanced Tracking Protection' option.
  3. Refresh the page if it didn't automatically refresh'.
For Brave Browser:
  1. Click the Brave Shields icon (often a lion head) to the right of the address bar.
  2. Toggle the 'Block fingerprinting' option.
  3. Refresh the page if it didn't automatically refresh'.

Rules

Nuances

1. Age Requirement (18+)

You must be 18 years of age or older to use this service. We do not allow anyone under the legal age on this platform. By creating an account, you affirm that you are a legal adult.

2. Not Safe For Work Content

  • We allow artistic freedom, including NSFW artwork, as long as it meets the platform’s standards.
  • Permitted Themes: Nudity, lewd imagery, and sexual acts may be depicted, provided they are created with effort and artistic quality in mind.
  • Quality Standards: NSFW art is judged by the same standards as all other art. Low-effort, spammy, or gratuitous explicit content may be removed.
  • Prohibited Content:
    • - Excessively graphic gore or hyper-realistic violence.
    - Hate imagery that disparages race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexuality, or disability.
    - Depictions or condoning of illegal acts
  • If you encounter violating art, after reporting it, consider creatively "fixing" it (e.g., adding clothing or a censor sticker) rather than erasing it entirely, if the underlying art is otherwise of quality.

3. Official SFW Zones & Content Restrictions

  • Respect Official SFW Zones: Areas officially designated as SFW must remain strictly Safe For Work and PG. This includes no explicit sexual content, no nudity, and no overtly suggestive imagery.
  • Suggestive & Fetish-Adjacent Content: Even if technically non-nude or "SFW," content that is clearly fetishistic, sexually charged, or intended to arouse may still be treated as suggestive and is not allowed in SFW zones.
  • Speech & Themes: Inflammatory, sexually provocative, or controversial statements are not permitted within SFW zones. Moderators may move or remove such content if it is deemed inappropriate for a general-audience area, at the discretion of the moderation team.
  • Zone Borders: The visual borders or markers that define SFW zones may be edited, decorated, or interacted with creatively. However, attempts to intentionally erase, mislead, or obscure, these borders are punishable.
  • Enforcement: Content violating SFW zone rules may be moved or deleted. Repeated or intentional violations may result in further moderation actions.

4. Griefing & Art Quality

  • We encourage high-quality, original pixel art. "Griefing" is defined as the malicious targeting of high-quality, original artwork with the intent to ruin it.
  • You are allowed to draw over low-effort creations. This includes large, simple flags that stand alone, repetitively spammed sprites, or single-color "voids." However, if an element is integrated into a larger, complex piece of art, the entire composition is protected.
  • Do not damage complex, original art just because you dislike it or its subject. Disputes over territory (e.g., flag wars) are not an excuse to grief well-made art.
  • As a collaborative canvas, edits and additions are expected and welcomed. However, edits that destroy the original intention of a high-quality piece are still considered griefing. And if another user removes your addition, respect their decision and avoid re-drawing it.

5. Harassment

  • Do not bully, harass, or target other users. Such as:
    • - Creating art with the intent to insult or threaten a specific user.
    - Organizing raids against a user's artwork.
    - Using the canvas to spread hateful messages about an individual.
  • If a user is breaking the rules, report them—do not take matters into your own hands.

6. External Linking

  • Explicit text links to Discord, YouTube and image gathering sites like Pixiv are allowed, provided they do not link to content that violates our rules or promotes prohibited activities.
  • Do not create QR codes, as they are harder to verify at a glance and are strictly banned.
  • All other links or forms of external referencing are subject to moderator judgment and will be removed in most cases.
  • For your safety, do not click on or attempt to access any links created by other users. We are not responsible for any harm caused by external content.

7. Reporting

  • Help us keep the canvas clean. If you see a rule violation, please report it.
  • Your report must include a screenshot that clearly shows the offending artwork and your cursor hovering over a pixel to display the username of the artist.
  • Intentionally filing false or frivolous reports (e.g., reporting a flag you simply don't like, reporting in retalliation) wastes moderator time and may result in action being taken against your account.

8. One Account Per Person

  • Only one account per person allowed.
  • No multi-accounting or bots. Do not create alternate accounts ("alts") or use automated scripts ("bots") to paint for any reason.
  • Attempts to circumvent result in permanent bans.

9. Intellectual Property

  • You are responsible for the art you create. By placing pixels, you affirm that your creation does not infringe on the intellectual property rights of others.
  • While we recognize the pixel art community often uses inspired designs, blatant copying of protected characters or logos is done at your own risk.
  • We are not liable for any copyright infringement conducted by users.
  • Reports of copyright infringement must include sufficient evidence as outlined in the Terms of Service. Unsubstantiated or bad-faith reports may be treated as false reports and can result in action against the reporting account.

10. Privacy

  • Do not share anyone's personal information, including your own.
  • This includes real names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, or any other private data.
  • Public online identities of your own are permitted, but are subject to the same scrutiny and rules as External Linking.
  • Reports of privacy or non-consensual content must be supported by sufficient evidence as described in the Terms of Service. False or malicious reports may result in moderation action against the reporting account.

11. Non-Consensual Real Life Content

  • Do not knowingly create or share depictions of real individuals without their explicit consent, especially in explicit, intimate, or privacy-violating scenarios.
  • This includes deepfakes, manipulated images, or identifiable drawings that could violate someone's privacy or dignity.
  • You are responsible for ensuring your content respects others' consent and privacy rights.

12. Exploits & Client Modification

  • Do not attempt to hack, exploit, or modify the website. This includes tampering with client-side JavaScript, CSS, or HTML to alter the page's functionality
  • Do not make automated, excessive, or malicious requests to our servers.
  • We are not responsible for any broken functionality, failed transactions, or other issues resulting from you modifying your client. Attempting to exploit the service will result in a ban.

13. Illegal Content

  • Do not create any artwork that violates applicable laws. This includes, but is not limited to:
    • - Hate speech targeting groups based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, or sexual orientation.
    - Content that promotes terrorism or violent extremism.
    - Any other material deemed illegal in your jurisdiction or ours.

14. Crypto, Web3, and Blockchain Content

  • Any content related to cryptocurrency, NFTs, blockchain, or Web3 technologies is strictly prohibited. This includes art, advertisements, text, or symbols referencing coins, tokens, decentralized platforms, or related social media communities.
  • Examples of prohibited content include (but are not limited to): logos or text referencing Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Dogecoin, NFTs, crypto exchanges, or decentralized services.
  • if such references appear only as small, non-prominent elements within an otherwise clearly high-quality artwork where crypto is not the focus or purpose, moderators may allow it on a case-by-case basis.
  • In all other cases, such content will be removed and the account permanently banned without warning.

15. Malicious Conduct & Platform Harm

In short: do not act in bad faith toward the platform, its infrastructure, its moderation team, or its reputation.

  • Do not engage in any behavior intended to harm, disrupt, undermine, misrepresent, or otherwise negatively impact the website, its services, its moderation systems, or its community.
  • This includes, but is not limited to:
    • - Attempting to overload, disrupt, or degrade the service (e.g., DDoS attacks, request flooding, stress testing without explicit permission).
    - Coordinating or encouraging others to interfere with the normal operation, availability, or stability of the site.
    - Abusing legal or reporting systems, including threats of legal action, mass reporting, or filing complaints in bad faith with the intent to intimidate, pressure, or disrupt the platform.
    - Acting in bad faith to "limit test" the rules, moderation team, or enforcement systems through deliberate borderline violations, loophole abuse, or repeated hostile challenges.
    - Harassing, threatening, impersonating, or targeting moderators or administrators, whether on the website, Discord, social media, or any other external platform, when such actions are connected to this service.
    - Intentionally spreading misinformation, false claims, or misleading narratives about the platform, its staff, or its policies, including on social media or other public forums, with the goal of damaging its reputation or provoking harassment.
  • Malicious actions are evaluated based on the full context known to the moderation team. This includes behavior, patterns, intent, coordination, and surrounding circumstances, whether the actions occur on the website or externally.
  • Legitimate criticism, feedback, or disagreement expressed in good faith is allowed. Actions taken dishonestly, deceptively, or with malicious intent are not.
  • These actions are considered severe violations of trust and community safety.
  • In nearly all cases, violations of this rule will result in an immediate and permanent ban, without warning.
